# Product

## Register

product

## Working title

**Settle** is the current user-facing product name (home screen, in-app copy,
notifications). The engineering codename and repository name remain
**PetCompanion**; bundle id, module name, and deep-link scheme are unchanged.

## Users

Settle is initially for first-time puppy owners, especially couples and
families coordinating care across a shared household. They use it in short,
frequent moments throughout the day to answer what their puppy needs now, what
another caregiver has already done, and what is coming next.

Experienced dog owners are a secondary audience who primarily need calm,
reliable organization. Trainers, veterinarians, breeders, walkers, sitters, and
other species are future audiences, not MVP scope.

## Product Purpose

Settle is the operating system for pet ownership. It turns a puppy's age,
development stage, household routines, care records, and progress into one
trustworthy Daily Plan. Success means owners feel guided rather than
overwhelmed, shared care is unambiguous, and the app remains useful throughout
the dog's life.

The primary task on any screen is to understand or record the next useful
action with as little friction as possible.

## Brand Personality

Calm, warm, and trustworthy. The voice is a knowledgeable friend: specific
without sounding clinical, encouraging without being excitable, and confident
without pretending certainty. The product should feel premium because it is
clear, restrained, and dependable.

## Anti-references

Settle must not resemble a cartoon pet game, a dense analytics dashboard,
an encyclopedia, a generic reminder app, or a social feed. Avoid puppy-themed
novelty decoration, paw-print wallpaper, rainbow category colors, streak
pressure, guilt mechanics, unexplained scores, overfilled dashboards,
glassmorphism, gratuitous animation, and clinical-looking medical software.

## Design Principles

1. **Today first.** Make the next useful action and its context immediately
   understandable.
2. **Guidance over information.** Help owners act; do not make them browse a
   library to discover what matters.
3. **Shared care without ambiguity.** Show what happened, who did it, and what
   remains without encouraging caregiver comparison.
4. **Trust through restraint.** Use clear provenance, honest system states, and
   explicit guidance boundaries. Never imply that unverified content or failed
   writes are authoritative.
5. **Built for a lifetime.** Keep the puppy experience focused without encoding
   puppy-only assumptions into the product foundation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support VoiceOver, Dynamic Type through the largest
accessibility sizes, 44-point minimum interactive targets, sufficient contrast
in light and dark modes, and complete non-color state cues. Respect Reduce
Motion and never require animation, sound, fine motor control, or caregiver
competition to understand progress. Use inclusive household language and
plain-language health boundaries.
