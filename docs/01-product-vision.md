# Product Vision

**Status:** Draft  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-26  
**Working product name:** PetCompanion

## Mission

Give every pet owner the confidence, guidance, and tools needed to raise a
healthy, happy, well-trained companion through personalized daily guidance and
lifelong care.

## Vision

PetCompanion becomes the central hub for every stage of pet ownership—from the
period before adoption through a pet's senior years.

Rather than acting as another library of pet information, PetCompanion
proactively tells owners what matters today, what is coming next, and how their
pet is progressing over time.

## Problem

New puppy owners must coordinate training, veterinary care, feeding, grooming,
socialization, appointments, supplies, and family responsibilities. Guidance is
fragmented across websites, videos, calendars, notes, reminders, and
conversations. Owners often do not know what to prioritize or whether another
caregiver has already completed a task.

PetCompanion brings that guidance and coordination into one calm, time-centric
experience.

## Positioning

PetCompanion is the operating system for pet ownership. Its first release is
purpose-built for puppy owners, while its foundation supports the pet's entire
life.

## Product principles

### Guidance over information

Help users act instead of asking them to browse an encyclopedia.

### Time-centric by default

Organize the experience around the pet's age, development stage, and current
needs.

### Reduce cognitive load

Make the next useful action obvious and make common logging tasks effortless.

### Shared care without ambiguity

Give each caregiver an individual account within a shared household so everyone
can see what has been completed, by whom, and what remains.

### Worth opening every day

Create a warm, calm, premium experience that becomes part of the household's
routine.

### Built for a lifetime

Focus the first release on puppies without embedding puppy-only assumptions in
the underlying product model.

## Core product promise

When an owner opens PetCompanion, they should quickly understand:

- What should I do today?
- What has already been done?
- What should I prepare for next?
- Is my puppy progressing appropriately?

## Initial product pillars

- **Care:** health, food, medication, grooming, veterinary records, and growth.
- **Training:** lessons, practice, progress, socialization, and achievements.
- **Life:** photos, memories, milestones, journal entries, and events.
- **Planner:** the Daily Plan, calendar, reminders, and recurring tasks.
- **Insights:** trends in growth, consistency, completion, and development.

## Initial scope boundary

Version 1 focuses on new dog owners and puppies. Support for other species,
professional accounts, artificial-intelligence coaching, wearables,
marketplaces, and veterinary integrations remains outside the initial release
unless later product discovery changes the scope.
