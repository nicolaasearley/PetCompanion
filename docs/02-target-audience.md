# Target Audience

**Status:** Draft  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-26

## Primary audience

First-time puppy owners who want clear guidance, consistent routines, and
confidence that they are not overlooking an important part of raising their
dog.

## Secondary audience

Experienced dog owners who already understand puppy care but want one organized
place for household coordination, schedules, records, training, and memories.

## Future audiences

- Breeders
- Trainers
- Veterinarians
- Groomers
- Dog walkers
- Pet sitters
